## About Oraneta

At **Oraneta**, we specialize in developing, implementing, and applying AI-powered solutions to solve real-world challenges for businesses of all types and sizes.

We help organizations harness the potential of AI to automate processes, improve operational efficiency, enhance customer experiences, and accelerate digital transformation.

## What We Do

### AI-Powered Solutions
We develop intelligent applications tailored to each company's needs.

### Intelligent Automation
We optimize business processes and eliminate repetitive tasks through artificial intelligence.

### Digital Assistants
We create intelligent assistants for customer service, sales, and internal operations.

### Enterprise Integration
We connect artificial intelligence with existing systems, platforms, and business processes.

### AI Consulting
We identify opportunities to apply artificial intelligence and generate measurable business value.
